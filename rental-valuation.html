What Affects Rental Value

What Determines What Your Property Could Rent For

Several factors influence a property's rental potential. We consider them together, alongside comparable local evidence.

Location

Where your property is situated, including the local area, transport links and demand from renters.

Property Type

Whether it is a flat, house, studio or room, and how it compares with similar local properties.

Bedrooms & Size

The number of bedrooms and bathrooms and the overall size and layout of the property.

Condition & Specification

The standard of finish, fixtures and general condition of the property.

Furnishing

Whether the property is furnished, part-furnished or unfurnished can influence its appeal.

Market Demand

Current demand in the local rental market and comparable rental evidence.
