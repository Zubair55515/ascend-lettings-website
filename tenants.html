Good to Know

Tenant Guidance

A little preparation makes the process smoother.

Documents You May Need

Photographic ID, proof of address, and proof of income or employment are commonly required for an application.

Referencing

Referencing usually covers identity, affordability, employment and previous landlord checks. We'll guide you through it.

Right to Rent

You'll need to demonstrate your right to rent in the UK. We'll let you know which documents are acceptable.
