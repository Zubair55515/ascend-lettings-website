# Ascend Lettings Brand Guidelines

*Version 1.0 — 2026*

These guidelines set out how to use the Ascend Lettings brand consistently across every touchpoint — website, social media, print and correspondence. Consistency builds trust, and trust is central to what Ascend Lettings offers.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

**Ascend Lettings** is a UK letting agency that helps landlords let their properties and helps tenants find a home, with a focus on clarity, professionalism and doing things properly.

- **Who we are:** A professional, service-led letting agency. We focus on letting — finding and referencing quality tenants and getting tenancies set up correctly. We are not a property management company and do not imply we provide property management services.
- **Brand personality:** Premium but approachable, trustworthy, organised, calm, honest.
- **Mission:** To make professional property letting simple, transparent and stress-free for landlords and tenants alike.
- **The "Ascend" idea:** The name and the ascending mark represent rising standards, upward progress and moving up — landlords growing their portfolios, tenants moving into a better home.

---

## 2. Brand Voice

Our voice is **professional, clear, confident, friendly, transparent and human.**

- Write in **British English** (e.g. *organise*, *licence*, *tenancy*, *enquiry*, *neighbourhood*).
- Be clear and direct. Short sentences. Plain words.
- Be warm and human — we are talking to real people about their home or their investment.
- Be confident and reassuring without over-promising.
- **Avoid corporate jargon**, buzzwords and filler ("synergy", "best-in-class", "leverage", "solutions-driven").
- Never invent statistics or make claims we cannot back up.
- Never imply we offer property management services.

**Say:** "We'll find and reference quality tenants and get your tenancy set up correctly."
**Not:** "Our best-in-class end-to-end solution leverages synergies to maximise ROI."

---

## 3. Logo

The primary logo pairs a clean wordmark ("Ascend" in Playfair Display, "Lettings" in spaced Inter) with an **abstract ascending mark** — three upward-stepping gold forms that suggest rising bars, growth and upward motion. It is deliberately not a literal house outline.

**Meaning:** The three ascending elements represent progress and moving up — the core "ascend" concept.

---

## 5. Logo Clear Space & Minimum Size

- **Clear space:** Keep clear space around the logo equal to the **height of the capital "A" in "Ascend"** on all sides. Keep other elements, text and edges outside this zone.
- **Minimum digital size:** **120px wide** for the full logo.
- **Minimum print size:** **30mm wide** for the full logo.
- The icon-only mark may be used smaller (e.g. 16px favicon) where the full logo is not legible.

---

## 6. Logo Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use the supplied logo files without modification.
- Place the logo on approved backgrounds (off-white, navy, charcoal, white, or clean imagery).
- Maintain the original proportions — always scale width and height together.
- Use the white/reversed logo on dark backgrounds and the primary logo on light backgrounds.

**Don't**
- Distort, stretch or squash the logo.
- Recolour the logo or the mark outside the approved palette.
- Add drop shadows, glows, outlines, gradients or other effects.
- Rotate the logo or rearrange its elements.
- Place the logo on clashing colours or busy backgrounds where it loses legibility.
- Recreate the logo in a different font.

## 8. Typography

**Primary — Playfair Display** (serif)
- Use for: headings H1–H3, and supporting/logo materials.
- Weights: 400 Regular, 700 Bold.

**Secondary — Inter** (sans-serif)
- Use for: body text, navigation, forms, small print.
- Weights: 300 Light, 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold.

**Heading scale**

| Level | Size |
|---|---|
| H1 | 56px |
| H2 | 40px |
| H3 | 32px |
| H4 | 24px |
| H5 | 20px |

**Body & spacing**
- Body: 18px Regular
- Small: 14px
- Line height: **1.6** for body, **1.2** for headings

Both fonts are free from Google Fonts and are already loaded on the website.

---

## 9. Iconography

- Use minimal, modern **line icons**.
- **2px stroke** weight.
- Colour icons **navy or gold** depending on background (white/gold on dark, navy/gold on light).
- Keep the style consistent — avoid mixing filled and line icon sets.

---

## 10. Photography Style

- Modern **UK interiors** and residential settings.
- **Natural light**, bright and airy.
- Professional quality — sharp, well-composed, uncluttered.
- Show **diverse tenants and landlords** authentically.
- Clean, calm and aspirational — never staged or stocky where it can be avoided.
- Avoid low-quality, dated or heavily filtered images.

## 12. Social Media Style

- Use the navy/gold palette consistently across every platform.
- The **brand icon (ascending mark) should always be present** — as the profile avatar and within templates.
- Keep a professional, helpful tone in captions; British English.
- Use the supplied templates in `brand/social/` for profiles, covers, posts and stories.
- Always keep the website URL visible on shareable graphics.

---

## 13. Digital Documents

- Use the **HTML letterhead template** (`brand/letterhead/letterhead-template.html`) for digital correspondence, and the SVG for print.
- **Always include the compliance footer** — ICO registration number and Property Redress Scheme membership number.
- Keep documents clean: navy header, gold accents, plenty of white space.

---

## 14. Do's

- Use the approved brand assets from the `brand/` folder.
- Maintain a consistent tone of voice across all channels.
- Always include compliance details (ICO, Property Redress Scheme) on business documents.
- Keep layouts clean, spacious and professional.
- Proofread in British English.

---

## 15. Don'ts

- **Never imply property management services** — Ascend Lettings is a letting agency.
- **Never invent statistics** or make unverifiable claims.
- **Never use unapproved colours or fonts.**
- Don't clutter designs or overuse gold — it is an accent, not a background.
- Don't alter, distort or recolour the logo.
